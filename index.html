How to Setup & Play

🔊 1. Listen
Press the play button to hear your word.
👀 2. Look
Look at the picture or clue if there is one.
🔤 3. Build
Choose the sound cards to make your word.
✅ 4. Check
Press Check when you are finished.
⭐ 5. Keep going
Have another try if you need to.
1. Create a game
Give your spelling game a name.
2. Add words
Add up to 20 words.
3. Add optional supports
Add a meaning, clue or picture to a word if helpful.
4. Build the sound cards
Break each word into the sound cards you want the child to use.
5. Choose colours
Each correct or extra card can use the colour you choose.
6. Preview
Test the word as the child will see it.
7. Save and play
Once you are happy with the game, it is ready to use.
