How to Setup & Play

🔊 1. Listen
Press the play button to hear your word.
👀 2. Look
Look at the picture if there is one.
🔤 3. Build
Choose the sound cards to make your word.
✅ 4. Check
Press Check when you are finished.
⭐ 5. Keep going
Have another try if you need to.
1. Create a game
Give your spelling game a name.
2. Add words
Add up to 20 words.
3. Build the sound cards
Break each word into the sound cards you want the child to use.
4. Choose colours
Each correct or extra card can use the colour you choose.
5. Add optional extras
Add extra cards, a picture and a recording if needed.
6. Preview
Test the word exactly as the child will see it.
7. Save and play
Once you are happy with the game, save it and it is ready to use.
